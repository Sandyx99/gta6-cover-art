Vice Coast Cover Studio — your photo, your caption: a GTA-style multi-panel cover, made offline in the browser and exported as a PNG to share. Six panel layouts, five Vice City sunset and neon colour grades, Anton title lettering with a Pacifico script subtitle, and one-click export in Square 1080 × 1080, Portrait 1080 × 1350 or Wide 1920 × 1080.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
